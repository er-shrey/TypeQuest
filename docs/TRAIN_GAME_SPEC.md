# Mini-game 2 — Word Train

**Goal for the child:** type a word, one letter per carriage, and the train
chugs off to the next station. Along the way the child learns to recognise
words and to spell them, not only to find keys.

It follows Rocket Launch: Rocket teaches "find this key", the train teaches
"type these keys in order", which is what typing is.

## Who it's for

Word Train unlocks once a child has **8 letters in play** from Rocket Launch.
Before that, the home card shows a friendly lock and how many letters to go.

## Screen

- A sunny day (a contrast to Rocket's night sky): clouds, rolling hills, a track.
- A friendly engine with Pip in the cab, pulling **one carriage per letter**.
- A **picture card** above the train (🐱) with the word's sound on tap.
- The on-screen keyboard along the bottom.

## One word

1. The train rolls in. Pip says the word ("Cat!"). Tapping the picture repeats it.
2. The next carriage to fill glows. **Correct key:** its letter pops in colour,
   the engine puffs smoke, a "chuff" plays, and Pip says the letter's name.
3. **Wrong key:** a small curious wobble and a soft "boop". Nothing is undone.
4. Word done: Pip spells and blends it, "C, A, T. Cat!", the whistle toots,
   and the train drives off. The next train rolls in with the next word.

## Seeing, then remembering (scaffolding)

| Mode | When | What the child sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **See it** | A new word, or one typed fewer than 2 times | Every letter on the carriages; the next one glows |
| **Remember it** | After 2 "See it" completions | Picture, spoken word and the **first letter** only; the rest are blank carriages |

Hidden letters always come back when a child is stuck, one step at a time:

1. **After 1 wrong key, or a pause** (5 s Gentle, 8 s Normal, 15 s Challenge),
   the missing letter appears faintly on its carriage.
2. **After 2 wrong keys, or a second pause**, its key glows with a bouncing
   arrow and Pip says the letter's name.

The child can always finish; nothing is reset. On Gentle the switch to
"Remember it" happens after 3 completions; on Challenge after 1. A word that
needed this much help twice in a row goes back to "See it" for its next round.

A word is **known** (gold star in the Word Book) after 2 "Remember it"
completions with no more than one hint, on different days.

## Lines and stations

100 stations on 4 lines. Each station is a short set of words: 3 on Normal,
2 on Gentle, 4 on Challenge.

| Line | Stations | Words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Little Line | 1–10 | 2 letters: up, go, hi, ox… |
| Rhyme Railway | 11–40 | 3 letters, grouped by rhyme family: cat, hat, bat → sun, bun |
| Forest Express | 41–70 | 4 letters, including blends and digraphs: fish, frog, ship, duck |
| Mountain Line | 71–100 | 5 letters: apple, house, train, tiger |

Each station brings at most 2 new words and fills the rest with words met
before, preferring ones not practised lately or found tricky. When choosing a
new word, the train prefers words made of letters the child already has in
play, allowing at most one new letter (its key glows). After station 100, the
train keeps running review stations.

## Rewards

Each station adds its new words to the child's **Word Book** (picture, word,
and a tap to hear it spelled). Known words get a gold star. Rewards are never
taken away. There is still no timer, score or speed measurement.

## Learning

Every letter typed is also recorded against that letter's skill, so practice
here counts for Rocket Launch too. Words are shown in the child's letter case
(CAT, cat, or Cat in the capital-case stage); typing is always
case-insensitive.
